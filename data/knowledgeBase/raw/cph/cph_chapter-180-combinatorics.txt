Combinatorics Combinatorics studies methods for counting combinations of objects. Usually, the goal is to ﬁnd a way to count the combinations efﬁciently without generating each combination separately. As an example, consider the problem of counting the number of ways to represent an integer n as a sum of positive integers. For example, there are 8 representations for 4: • 1+1+1+1 • 1+1+2 • 1+2+1 • 2+1+1 • 2+2 • 3+1 • 1+3 • 4 A combinatorial problem can often be solved using a recursive function.
