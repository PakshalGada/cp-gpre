Method 2 Another way to solve the problem is based on a tree traversal array1. Once again, the idea is to traverse the nodes using a depth-ﬁrst search: However, we use a different tree traversal array than before: we add each node to the array always when the depth-ﬁrst search walks through the node, and not only at the ﬁrst visit. Hence, a node that has k children appears k +1 times in the array and there are a total of 2n−1 nodes in the array. 1This lowest common ancestor algorithm was presented in [7]. This technique is sometimes called the Euler tour technique [66].
