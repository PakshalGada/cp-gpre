The priority queue should contain all nodes that can be connected to the current component using a single edge, in increasing order of the weights of the corresponding edges. The time complexity of Prim’s algorithm is O(n+mlogm) that equals the time complexity of Dijkstra’s algorithm. In practice, Prim’s and Kruskal’s algorithms are both efﬁcient, and the choice of the algorithm is a matter of taste. Still, most competitive programmers use Kruskal’s algorithm.
