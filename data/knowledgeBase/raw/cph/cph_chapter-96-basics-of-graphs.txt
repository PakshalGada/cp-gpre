Basics of graphs Many programming problems can be solved by modeling the problem as a graph problem and using an appropriate graph algorithm. A typical example of a graph is a network of roads and cities in a country. Sometimes, though, the graph is hidden in the problem and it may be difﬁcult to detect it. This part of the book discusses graph algorithms, especially focusing on topics that are important in competitive programming. In this chapter, we go through concepts related to graphs, and study different ways to represent graphs in algorithms. Graph terminology A graph consists of nodes and edges.
