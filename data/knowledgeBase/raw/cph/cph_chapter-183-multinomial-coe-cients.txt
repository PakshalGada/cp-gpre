Multinomial coeﬃcients The multinomial coefﬁcient Ã n k1,k2,...,km ! = n! k1!k2!···km!, equals the number of ways we can divide n elements into subsets of sizes k1,k2,...,km, where k1 +k2 +···+km = n. Multinomial coefﬁcients can be seen as a generalization of binomial cofﬁcients; if m = 2, the above formula corresponds to the binomial coefﬁcient formula.
