Maximum matchings The maximum matching problem asks to ﬁnd a maximum-size set of node pairs in an undirected graph such that each pair is connected with an edge and each node belongs to at most one pair. There are polynomial algorithms for ﬁnding maximum matchings in general graphs [17], but such algorithms are complex and rarely seen in programming contests. However, in bipartite graphs, the maximum matching problem is much easier to solve, because we can reduce it to the maximum ﬂow problem.
